EmitMail is a high-performance, easy-to-use email infrastructure solution designed to give engineering teams total control over their email deliverability, data privacy, and costs. Built on **BullMQ**, **Redis**, and modern cryptographic isolation with **DKIM (2048-bit)**, **SPF**, and **DMARC**.

## Why Choose EmitMail?

- **Simplicity**: 100% drop-in compatibility with the official Resend SDK—migrate your app by simply changing `baseUrl`.
- **Flexibility**: Complete multi-tenant domain isolation, granular Role-Based Access Control (`ADMIN`, `MEMBER`, `VIEWER`), and unified inbox.
- **Open Source & Sovereign**: Free and self-hostable on your own VPS with zero per-email fees or artificial deliverability throttle.
